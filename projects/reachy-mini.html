Project Detail

Reachy Mini Conversational Check-in

Human-Robot Interaction · Mar – Jun 2026

PythonLLMHuman-Robot InteractionPrompt Design

This project explored a short conversational check-in experience using a Reachy Mini robot. The goal was to design dialogue that could invite users to reflect on their emotional state in a natural and approachable way.

← Back to all projects

My Contributions

  • Designed dialogue flow and system prompts for a short emotional check-in.
  • Developed alternative response styles, including neutral and empathetic modes.
  • Integrated the dialogue design with a Python-based interaction pipeline and the robot demonstration.
  • Supported demo testing, participant interaction, and analysis of feedback and observations.

Focus

The work combined interaction design and language-model behavior with a physical conversational interface. It strengthened my interest in human-AI interaction and how system behavior shapes a user's experience.
