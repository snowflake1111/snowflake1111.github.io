Hermes Microbiome Analysis Platform

Research Internship · Jul – Aug 2026

PythonMicrobiome16S rRNAWorkflow EngineeringReporting

Hermes is a natural-language-driven workflow for microbiome analysis with human-in-the-loop review. During my summer research internship at Academia Sinica, I contributed to parts of the 16S rRNA analysis workflow and its supporting interface.

← Back to all projects

Workflow

The workflow supports common 16S rRNA analysis stages, including preprocessing and quality control, taxonomy and abundance analysis, alpha/beta diversity, ordination, group comparisons, and report generation.

My Contributions

  • Implemented and tested a complete analysis task within the workflow.
  • Worked on MCP tools and skills used to connect analysis capabilities.
  • Contributed to the Analysis Hub and report customization.
  • Improved monitoring UI elements to make workflow progress easier to inspect.
  • Worked on short-read and long-read 16S workflows.

Design Considerations

The work focused on making analysis steps inspectable and useful to researchers, rather than treating the workflow as a black box. Human review and clear reporting were important parts of the experience.
