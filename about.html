Profile

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Information Engineering at National Taiwan Normal University, with academic experience at Uppsala University in Sweden. My interests span brain-computer interfaces, neural signal analysis, machine learning, and biomedical applications.

Through my EEG research, I have explored how signal preprocessing affects downstream task performance. I am interested in neural decoding, computational approaches to neuroscience, and the development of intelligent systems for biomedical and human-centered applications.

My experience also includes microbiome analysis workflow development, human-robot interaction, and software quality assurance. These projects have allowed me to work across research and engineering settings while developing my interdisciplinary perspective.
